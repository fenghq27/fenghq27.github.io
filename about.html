Sample introduction

I’m Sophie.

I wanted somewhere to put all the little things I tend to save: photographs, half-filled notebooks, favorite lines from books, and notes about what made a Tuesday feel special.

So I imagined a corner shop with a bell over the door and shelves full of memories. This is that shop. Feel free to wander, pull out a photo, or read a page from the notebook.

With love,
Sophie ♡
